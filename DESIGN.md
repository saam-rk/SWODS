# Common Light — public edition

## Direction

SWODS is a mobile-first collective photography prototype. The public edition retains its warm working surfaces, square mosaic geometry and short interactions, but replaces all supplied raster artwork with an original **geometric contact sheet**. The signature is a field of differently framed observations: diagonals, concentric circles and horizontal reflections assembled into one composition. These are abstract graphics, not photographs of Hervás or evidence of real participants.

The change is intentionally confined to the environmental artwork. Navigation, capture, galleries, administration, presentation and memory retain the existing component architecture.

`styles/tokens.css` remains authoritative for the complete palette, spacing, motion and safe-area tokens. The 4px spacing scale and tight corners distinguish working planes from photography. Body text is 16px; controls have at least 44px touch targets. Dense text stays on controlled paper or inverse surfaces, not directly over artwork.

## Typography

Apfel Grotezk 400 and 700, installed through Fontsource, supplies display and interface roles. Scale and weight provide hierarchy without introducing another downloaded font. The font is licensed under SIL OFL; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the complete OFL text under `licenses/`.

`public/shared-field.svg` fills the landing and operating-route perimeter with `cover` cropping. One small vector file serves mobile and desktop; no mobile raster companion is needed. On phones the bottom navigation reserves safe-area space and forms stack; desktop reflows participant/admin work without stretching a narrow phone column. The landing keeps its existing single entrance; reduced motion removes it.

## Media rules

- The six shipped assets are original deterministic geometry generated by `scripts/generate-public-assets.mjs`; `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` records their provenance and checksums.
- The demo target is a synthetic landscape diagram, not a photograph or brand asset.
- Fictional gallery fixtures contain no photograph URLs. Neutral placeholders are intentional until a user supplies their own permitted image.
- Do not use abstract artwork to claim that participants submitted images or that the activity took place.
- Do not restore the old collage files, campaign media or old screenshots.
- New portfolio screenshots must be captured from clean local demo contexts with synthetic images, not real accounts.

## Accessibility and motion

Maintain visible focus, labels, error associations, keyboard access, dialog focus restoration, live status messages, 44px targets and reduced-motion behavior. The growing mosaic rail communicates collective progress rather than competition. The reveal is the only cinematic sequence and includes a skip action. Automated browser checks are useful regressions, not a WCAG certification; manual device and assistive-technology acceptance remains required.
